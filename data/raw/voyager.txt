TITLE: Voyager Mission
Voyager 1 and its twin Voyager 2 are the only spacecraft ever to operate outside the heliosphere, the protective bubble of particles and magnetic fields generated by the Sun. Voyager 1 reached the interstellar boundary in 2012, while Voyager 2 (traveling slower and in a different direction than its twin) reached it in 2018. Now Voyager 1 is on the brink of another unprecedented milestone — reaching a distance one light-day away from Earth.
